SpeakDaba Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

SpeakDaba is a language-exchange application that helps users connect with others and practice languages.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, age, country, native language, learning language, language level, profile photo, bio, and interests.

We may also process account information, messages, and technical information needed to operate and secure the application.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, display profiles, help users find language partners, enable messaging, maintain security, and improve the application.

3. Messages and User Content

Information that users choose to share through profiles or messages may be visible to other users. Please do not share sensitive personal information with people you do not know.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the application, such as authentication, database, or hosting providers, where applicable. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the security of users and the service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the service and meet applicable legal or security requirements. Users may contact us to request access to or deletion of their personal information.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

SpeakDaba is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the SpeakDaba app developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
