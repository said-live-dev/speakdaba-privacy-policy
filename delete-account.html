Delete Your SpeakDaba Account

SpeakDaba users can request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion, contact the SpeakDaba support team using the contact information provided in the app's Privacy Policy. Include your account email address and state that you want your SpeakDaba account deleted.

What data will be deleted?

We intend to delete your account information and associated personal data, including your profile and other personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the required period.

Processing your request

We will verify your request and provide information about its processing. Account deletion is not automatic through this webpage.

For more information, please read our Privacy Policy .
